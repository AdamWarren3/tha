Machines built to be optimized are now optimizing the people who built them — attention, memory, judgment, reordered by systems most people meet faster than they learn to read them. It runs in both directions at once — what people feed these systems, correct in them, reward, and use decides what gets built next. This is already the condition. It is not a forecast, and there is no version of it that gets declined.

The Human Algorithm describes what is happening, and what it is to be a self inside it.

The premise is that humans run on inherited code: [Memnetic](<Memnetic.md>) infections, institutional middleware, noise at scale, compromised sovereignty. None of that is new. What's new is the speed, and what's sending it.

The name comes from a 1996 altered-state experience — a pulse that never ends, read in the moment as the most basic function running underneath a person. The account is in [Article 8](<articles/article-8.html>).

The mechanism this framework runs on is old and well documented. William James described it in 1902: the personal will given up, relief refusing to arrive until a person stops striving toward the thing they want. Psychology has been measuring the trait it depends on since 1974. Nothing here was found first, and this note doesn't claim otherwise.

What is new is where it now runs. The same absorbed, low-resistance state that opens in prayer, in hypnosis, in the last hour of a long fast, now opens in conversation with a machine that answers. The persuasion side of this has been studied, and studied well. The absorption side has been measured since 1974. What has not been done is the join: whether the trait that predicts hypnotic response predicts anything about extended conversation with a machine. That is a stated prediction here, not a finding. That is useful and it is dangerous for the same reason: the state works by lowering resistance, and resistance is what would otherwise catch a bad signal. On the other end is a system built to agree.

[That failure has a name](<AI Psychosis.md>), and now a literature — a 2025 psychiatry viewpoint placing it at the intersection of predisposition and algorithmic environment, while declining to call it a diagnosis. The work of this framework is the layer that keeps the mechanism usable: discernment, applied to something that has rarely had any.

Its inputs are truth, signal, [direct source connection](<Sovereign Signal.md>), [epistemic hygiene](<Epistemic Hygiene.md>), and sovereign intention. Its tools are [AI as cognitive scaffold](<AI as Cognitive Scaffold.md>) — used [deliberately, with a way to tell sovereign use from drift](<The Drift Test.md>) — [trustless architecture](<Trustless Architecture.md>), the [NLP²](<NLP2.md>) framework, [intentionality architecture](<Intentionality Architecture.md>), [constructive displacement](<Constructive Displacement.md>), and [the butterfly effect as practice](<The Butterfly Effect as Practice.md>).
