The discipline of knowing how you know what you know. [[The Human Algorithm]] runs on accurate inputs — [[Signal vs Slop|garbage in, garbage out]] applies to human cognition as much as machine learning.

Epistemic hygiene means: distinguishing source from signal, verified from assumed, [[The Optimization Paradox|pattern from proof]]. Holding beliefs at the correct confidence level — not too tight, not too loose.

AI hallucination — where models generate confident, plausible, unverified output — is a significant epistemic risk in the current information environment. A model that doesn't know it doesn't know something is more dangerous than one that admits uncertainty. The output sounds authoritative. It circulates as fact. The burden of verification falls entirely on the user.

**A label is not a substitute for the practice, and the reason is measured.** Source tags on advertising messages were forgotten after a short period, and attributions were afterwards reconstructed by schematic guessing from how credible the claim already seemed — leading the authors to conclude that labelling untrustworthy sources is of limited use, because the label does not survive in memory even though the content does [S47]. In the same literature, participants remembered social media content better than traditional news content while the source it carried had no effect on memory performance at all. The direct test has now been run: after working with a model, people were measurably worse at remembering which ideas had been their own, and worst of all where the work was mixed [S56]. The implication for disclosure of AI-generated output is direct and unwelcome: a person can read the label, believe it, and retain the claim without it. Provenance has to be re-established at the point of use, which is what this practice is, rather than delivered once at the point of contact.

In an AI-saturated information environment, epistemic hygiene is not academic. It is survival.

The optimizer is only as good as the data it trains on. You are the data.
