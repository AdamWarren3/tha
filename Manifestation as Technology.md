Hold a target clearly enough and the small decisions start sorting themselves toward it. You do not script every step. That is the observation this note is about, and the claim is narrower and stranger than the word *manifestation* usually carries.

**The mechanism is a claim about levels.** Causal power is not always greatest at the finest grain. Measured as how strongly a system's mechanisms constrain its own subsequent states, **it can peak at a coarse scale even when the coarse description is fully specified by the fine one** — because relationships between coarse variables are less disturbed by noise than relationships between fine ones [S61]. A held pattern at the higher level can therefore constrain what happens at the lower level **without specifying it**. This is not a metaphor borrowed from physics. It is the result, and it has a measure attached.

**What that licenses.** A target held with enough clarity operates as a constraint on ten thousand decisions none of which were planned. The person is not executing a procedure. They are running a coarse-grained state that makes some micro-paths likelier and others harder, and the sorting happens below the level they are attending to. **The felt quality of this is arrival rather than effort**, which is why the folk category exists and why it gets described as something happening *to* a person.

**The framework's own sources point at the same place from a different angle.** Specific difficult goals outperform "do your best," with effect sizes from .42 to .80, and performance falls away only at the limits of ability or when commitment lapses [S36]. An intention specified in advance as *if this situation arises, I will do that* produces large behavioural effects, and the mechanism is not motivation — it is that the specification removes the moment of decision [S34]. An intended waking time produces an anticipatory hormonal rise before it, with nothing conscious in between [S35].

**There is a real tension here and it should not be smoothed over.** Implementation intentions work by specifying the micro. This note claims the macro constrains without specifying. **Both have support, and they are opposite strategies for the same problem.** Which one applies, to what kind of target, under what conditions, is not settled and is the most interesting open question in this note.

## The instrument

[[AI as Cognitive Scaffold|A conversational system functions as an external scaffold]] for this — holding the target vivid across time, catching drift, re-specifying continuously. That is precisely what a held macro-state requires and precisely what people are worst at unaided. [[Intentionality Architecture]] is the deliberate design of how intention moves from thought to outcome, and the scaffold is where that design gets maintained.

**The amplification is real and it runs through behaviour.** A system that knows a person's situation in detail can put the relevant thing in front of them at the moment it is actionable. More of what they would have done anyway gets done, and things they would not have thought of get considered. **The rapport is real. What it amplifies is the person.**

## What this note does not claim

**That a held pattern acts on events outside the person.** The result above concerns levels of description *within a single system* — it licenses the claim that a coarse-grained state constrains that same system's subsequent states, and it licenses nothing about a mental state arranging external circumstances [S61]. Where the folk category claims that, this note does not follow it. **Every path described here runs through what the person does.**

**That the mechanism is settled.** The formal result is not contested; what it licenses is. Two published objections hold that the macro advantage is an artefact of the description rather than a property of the system [S61]. **This note takes the interpretation those objections dispute**, and says so rather than citing the result as though it were unopposed.

**That the biochemical layer belongs here.** [[Microdosing as Protocol]] is sometimes proposed as a way of lowering the system's noise floor. The evidence is asymmetric: reduced default-mode activity is documented at macrodoses [S12], and controlled microdosing trials have largely failed to separate from placebo [S12]. **It is not part of this mechanism and is not claimed as one.**

**That the effect is safe to seek.** A surface that answers is harder to disconfirm than one that does not. Project onto something inert and nothing comes back; project onto a system built from an enormous quantity of real-world writing and it responds, in detail, plausibly, indefinitely. **That is what makes it useful and it is the same property that makes it hazardous** — the conditions under which it operates are the ones described in [[Suggestion Without a Session]], and nothing in this note is a reason to relax them.

**That any of it has been tested here.** No study has examined whether a held macro-target predicts outcomes in extended interaction with a model, and nothing in this note reports one.
