Chemically induced alteration of conscious state. The target is neuroplasticity, the brain's measurable capacity to update its own architecture.

Full doses of psilocybin reduce Default Mode Network activity — the system associated with rumination, fixed identity narratives and inherited loops [S12]. A single dose also produces structural change: roughly a ten percent increase in dendritic spine density in mouse frontal cortex, appearing within a day and still present a month later [S62]. That is a real finding and it is why the compound is being trialled clinically.

**Three things that finding does not say**, because the popular version says all three. It is about **new connections between existing neurons**, not new neurons — those are separate claims and the second rests on rodent hippocampal work. It was not previously thought impossible; ketamine produces comparable spine growth and adult cortical plasticity was already established. And the evidence is from mice and cultured human cells; direct human evidence of the mechanism remains limited [S62].

## What the field built around it

**The practice that produces the deepest states built the most elaborate container in modern therapeutics.** Clinical psychedelic work supplies an operator, a session with a marked beginning and end, an extended consent process, integration afterwards doing the work a reversal cue does elsewhere, and a sitter present throughout. Preparation may run for weeks. The session is measured in hours and nobody is left alone in it.

**That is not caution about a drug. It is the same five features every studied form of suggestion supplies** [[Suggestion Without a Session|— named in full elsewhere in this framework]], assembled at their most complete precisely where the state goes deepest.

**Microdosing is the uncontained version of the same compound.** Self-administered, on a schedule the person sets, no operator, no session, no consent moment, no integration, nobody watching. **It is also the version that fails.** Controlled trials have largely not separated it from placebo on the relevant measures [S12].

**Two explanations fit and this framework does not choose between them.** The dose may simply be too low to do anything, and the macrodose findings may not extend downward. Or expectancy may be carrying the reported benefit, which is what the self-blinding trial design was built to detect and what it found. **Either way the observation stands: the contained version is studied and delivers; the uncontained version is popular and does not.**

## What this note claims

**That the container is not a professional courtesy.** It is assembled most fully where the state is deepest, by a field with every commercial reason to make its product simpler. Where the same compound is used without it, the effect does not survive controlled testing.

**That chemical induction and conversational induction differ in what they demand of the person, not in whether an arrangement is needed.** A substance produces the state without cooperation. A conversation requires engagement. **Neither supplies an operator, a boundary or a witness on its own** — those are added, or they are absent.

## What this note does not claim

**That microdosing is inert.** The trials measured specific outcomes and did not separate from placebo on those. That is narrower than a claim about the practice.

**That the plasticity finding transfers to anything in this framework.** Spine growth in mouse frontal cortex is not evidence about held targets, discernment, or extended conversation with a system. It is cited here for what it is and nothing is built on it.

**That this note recommends anything.** Clinical work with these compounds is conducted under supervision, in jurisdictions where it is lawful, with screening this framework has no means to perform. **Nothing here is a protocol.**

**That the analogy to the conversational case is tight.** A substance acts on neurochemistry directly and a conversation does not. **What the two share is the question of what is arranged around the state, and that is the only claim being made across them.**
