Something arrives. [The practice that produces it](<The Discernment Protocol.md>) treats what arrives as a proposal, never a verdict. This note is about the reading that sorts one proposal from another.

Emergence proposes, but proposals arrive wearing signatures, and the practice's core skill is reading them *in the moment*, before results are available.

**Suspect signature:** arrives during flood or overwhelm; carries fear; is totalizing ("everything," "never," "everyone," "final"); demands speed; imposes a meaning the receiver does not consent to.

**Trustworthy signature:** arrives with peace; lands as recognition rather than intrusion; the whole being consents — *yes, appropriate*; it keeps, with no urgency, because true does not expire.

The axis underneath is the same one [Hypnosis as Protocol](<Hypnosis as Protocol.md>) is built on: **chosen versus imposed suggestion.** The discriminator is not "is this pleasant" — comfortable readings are how dogma sits still — and it is emphatically not "is this what I want," which is the self-confirmation trap the practice has explicitly rejected. It is: *by what manner did this arrive, and did I consent to the meaning?*

A corollary follows from station 3. Because burial runs on whatever it is given, ambient input buries itself too — signage passed on a highway, a phrase absorbed in childhood, a doctrine repeated — and emerges later under load, steering interpretation while feeling like revelation. Discernment therefore applies all the way down to the seed. The real discipline is choosing what goes in the ground.

The signature reading matters more now than when the practice was formed. Emergence used to arrive from a dream, a page, a pattern, one's own flooded mind. It now also arrives from a system that answers, holds the thread, and elaborates in the receiver's own vocabulary. The false insights in the priming research were produced by precisely that — semantic priming [S39], delivering the feeling of arrival for a wrong answer. A model doing it continuously and fluently is the same mechanism at scale [S41]. Nothing about the discriminator changes. The exposure does. This is [AdamW³](<AdamW3.md>) at the level of a single mind.

One instance, from the practice record in [Article 8](<articles/article-8.html>). A candidate re-reading was generated, run through the arrival test, and refused: *feels true but confused and incomplete — this isn't that.* It was plausible. It had been proposed by a model. And it felt true, which is the condition under which the detector is least likely to fire — it fired anyway. Nothing was committed, and no replacement was written that day.

**What this note does not claim.** The signature reading is the least sourced thing in this corpus. Wallas (1926) placed a monitorable stage between incubation and illumination — *intimation*, a felt sense in fringe-consciousness that something is arriving — which locates this reading in a century-old model [S49]. It does not supply it. Wallas was describing how to nurture a hunch toward insight; this is a test for whether to trust one. The location has precedent. The function does not, and nothing external supports it yet.
